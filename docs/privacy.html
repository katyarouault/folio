Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026

Folio helps you scan, draw and organize your handwritten notes. This policy explains what information Folio collects and how it is used.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name and email address. If you sign up with email, you also choose a password, which is stored securely and is never visible to us.
  • Sign in with Apple or Google: if you use one of these, we receive the name and email address that Apple or Google shares with us. With Apple you can choose to hide your email, and we then receive a private relay address instead.
  • Your library: the notes, folders, tags, photos, drawings, to-do lists and profile photo you add to Folio.
  • Technical information needed to keep you signed in.

How we use it

  • To create your account, sign you in, and email you codes to confirm your email or reset your password.
  • To save your library so you can see it on any device where you sign in.
  • To reply when you contact support.

We do not sell your information, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites.

Camera and photos

Folio asks to use your camera and photo library only so you can scan pages or choose photos of your notes. It only accesses the photos you pick.

Where it is stored

Your account and library are stored with Supabase, our database provider. Account emails are sent through Google (Gmail). A copy of your library is also kept on your device so Folio works offline. Your notes and photos are private to your account; other Folio users cannot see them.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time in Folio under Profile → Account Settings → Delete my account. This permanently removes your account, notes, folders and photos. You can also email us to ask for deletion.

Children

Folio is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
