Common questions

I didn't get my code

Codes can take a minute to arrive. Check your spam or junk folder, then tap Send a new code.

I forgot my password

On Log in, tap Forgot password?, enter your email, and type in the 6-digit code we send you. If you signed up with Apple or Google, use that button instead; there's no separate Folio password.

Where are my notes saved?

In your private Folio account, so they're there when you sign in on another device. A copy also stays on your phone so Folio works offline.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile → Account Settings → Delete my account. This permanently removes your account, notes and photos.

How do I get the clearest scans?

Lay the page flat in good light, fit the corners to the page, then use Enhance → Cleaned to whiten the paper and sharpen the writing.
